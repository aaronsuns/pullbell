Pullbell / Ready-to-merge notification

Get a notification when your pull request is ready to merge

You opened the PR, asked for a review, and now you keep checking: approved yet? Checks done? Pullbell tells you when it can be merged, and when it cannot.

What counts as ready to merge

Pullbell rings 🚀 Ready to merge when your pull request is approved, its checks are green, it has no conflict and it is not a draft. All four must be true, so the banner means you can click Merge now.

Conflicts ring too

When your PR just got a merge conflict, Pullbell rings ⚠️ Conflict so it does not sit unnoticed after someone else merges first.

Other things that ring for your own PRs

  • ✅ your PR is approved
  • ❌ a review requests changes
  • 💬 a person comments on it, or replies in a thread you took part in

A burst of comments on one PR is one banner. Your own comments, reviews and pushes never ring.

See all your PRs at once

Press ⌃⌘P for My PRs: first the PRs waiting for your review, then your own open PRs with a CI and review glyph for each. Every kind of banner can be switched to the quiet FYI list in Settings, then Alerts.

Frequently asked questions

When does Pullbell say a PR is ready to merge?

When it is approved, checks are green, there is no merge conflict and it is not a draft.

Does it notify me about my own pushes?

No. Your own comments, reviews and pushes never ring.

Can I turn off one kind of banner?

Yes. In Settings, then Alerts, any kind can be moved to the quiet FYI list.

How often does it check GitHub?

Every 3 minutes by default. You can choose 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes.
